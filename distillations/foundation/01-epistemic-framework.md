## 1. Thesis

Because we are made of the system we study, we cannot observe its Outside. The framework is therefore agnostic about the Architect's nature and the ontology of consciousness, and treats human contingency and the rejection of cosmic privilege as methodological commitments. Fine-tuning grounds a probable but fallible inference to a non-intervening Architect, preferred over observer selection, the multiverse and brute fact on grounds of explanatory content. The Infinite Pool is energy seen as shared participation: an interpretive frame that is neither new physics, a consciousness field nor a source of moral obligation.

- **Claim:** Because we are composed of the matter and code we seek to understand, we are fundamentally unable to observe the Outside or the Source of the system.

## 9. Drift and open questions

- **§1 type label:** the chapter states that everything in §1 is "methodological commitments, not empirical claims", and most §1 claims are typed that way. By ruling L-0001 (B), four claims are instead typed by their content: AD-I-009 and AD-I-010 as Inference, AD-I-011 as Empirical and AD-I-012 as Postulated.
- **AD-I-011 and the Preamble:** AD-I-011 (Empirical) restates AD-P-008, the Blind Optimizer, which is also Empirical; this matches the glossary's "Established science".
- **Preamble typing:** the Preamble gives no type labels. Its restated claims were retyped to match Chapter I: AD-P-009 (humanity is contingent, not the culmination) is now Inference, restated by AD-I-009; AD-P-010 (humanity is not the point of existence) is now Methodological, restated by AD-I-013. AD-P-011 (humans are temporary and replaceable) stays Inference.
- **AD-I-008:** acknowledging that the Architect may exist sits under the §1 methodological label, but §2 makes the Architect an inference (AD-I-018, AD-I-029).
- **AD-I-023 (Global Variable):** typed Interpretation, following Book XI.2 ("fine-tuning" as calibration is an interpretation), although the wording "suggests … a designer" reads as an inference.
- **AD-I-024 (alternative explanations):** typed Uncertainty. It could also be read as a methodological acknowledgement.
- **Candidate foundation-internal tension (no ledger entry opened):** §3 frames the Pool as "a pervasive field of potential that animates biological hardware" (AD-I-031), then says it "simply isn't a physical field in the scientific sense" (AD-I-035). "Animates" also edges towards vitalism. Opening a ledger entry is outside distillation; this awaits your decision.
- **L147** refers to earlier versions of the framework that used "Field" in its name. Only the substantive point is distilled (AD-I-035), not the version history.
