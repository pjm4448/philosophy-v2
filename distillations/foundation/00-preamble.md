# Preamble

## 1. Thesis

Agnostic Deism is a structure for living, not a religion or a scientific theory. It accepts hard limits on knowledge, infers a non-intervening Architect who authored laws rather than outcomes, and denies humanity any cosmic privilege. Meaning and ethics are therefore constructed and chosen, with finite solidarity at their centre. Consciousness is held as real but ontologically open, death as the end of the individual, and every claim is held to the standard of its own epistemic type.

## 2. Claims

### Epistemology

#### AD-P-001 — The framework is a structure for living
- **Claim:** The framework is a structure for interpreting and living within existence, not a religion, a scientific theory or a claim to revealed truth.
- **Type:** Methodological
- **Concepts:** agnostic-deism, revelation
- **Source:** § Preamble (L11)
- **Quote:** "It is not a religion, not a scientific theory, and not a claim to revealed truth."

#### AD-P-002 — Knowledge from within the system is fundamentally limited
- **Claim:** There are fundamental limits on what can be known from within the system: the Outside cannot be observed, the Architect's nature cannot be verified and cosmic truth is not accessible.
- **Type:** Methodological
- **Concepts:** agnosticism
- **Source:** § Preamble, The Name Explained: Agnostic (L14)

#### AD-P-003 — Epistemic humility is foundational
- **Claim:** Because of these limits, epistemic humility is foundational to the framework.
- **Type:** Methodological
- **Concepts:** agnosticism, epistemic-status
- **Source:** § Preamble, The Name Explained: Agnostic (L14)
- **Quote:** "Epistemic humility is therefore foundational."

#### AD-P-035 — Claim types are distinct and held to their own standards
- **Claim:** The framework's claims are of different kinds (empirical foundations, philosophical inferences, metaphysical interpretations, postulated models, methodological commitments and constructed values); these are not interchangeable, and each must be held to the standard appropriate to it.
- **Type:** Methodological
- **Concepts:** epistemic-status
- **Source:** § Preamble, Epistemic Discipline (L32)
- **Quote:** "An evocative metaphor is not thereby physics; an inference is not an observation"

#### AD-P-036 — A scientific fact does not by itself produce a moral obligation
- **Claim:** A scientific fact does not, by itself, produce a moral obligation.
- **Type:** Methodological
- **Concepts:** is-ought-gap, epistemic-status
- **Source:** § Preamble, Epistemic Discipline (L32)

#### AD-P-037 — A chosen value is not dishonest for being uncommanded
- **Claim:** A chosen value is not made dishonest merely because the universe did not command it.
- **Type:** Methodological
- **Concepts:** constructed-ethics, moral-construction
- **Source:** § Preamble, Epistemic Discipline (L32)

#### AD-P-038 — Agnostic Deism is the metaphysical foundation, not the whole philosophy
- **Claim:** Agnostic Deism is the metaphysical foundation of a broader philosophy rather than a complete description of everything the framework contains.
- **Type:** Methodological
- **Concepts:** agnostic-deism
- **Source:** § Preamble (L34)

#### AD-P-039 — The philosophy confronts existence without the traditional supports
- **Claim:** The broader philosophy attempts to confront existence without revelation, humanity without cosmic privilege, morality without divine command, suffering without providence, agency without guaranteed free will and mortality without an afterlife.
- **Type:** Methodological
- **Concepts:** revelation, anthropocentrism, constructed-ethics, problem-of-evil, free-will, mortality
- **Source:** § Preamble (L34)

### Architect and metaphysics

#### AD-P-004 — A non-intervening Architect is inferred from apparent design
- **Claim:** A non-intervening Architect is inferred from features of reality interpreted as suggestive of design, particularly the apparent fine-tuning of physical constants, while explicitly accepting that the inference may be wrong.
- **Type:** Inference
- **Concepts:** architect, deism, fine-tuning
- **Source:** § Preamble, The Name Explained: Deism (L16)
- **Quote:** "We infer a non-intervening Architect from features of reality we interpret as suggestive of design"

#### AD-P-005 — The Architect does not intervene, answer prayer, reveal law or manage outcomes
- **Claim:** Unlike the God of classical theism, the Architect does not intervene, answer prayers, reveal moral law or manage particular outcomes.
- **Type:** Inference
- **Concepts:** architect, deism, revelation
- **Source:** § Preamble, The Name Explained: Deism (L16)

#### AD-P-007 — The Architect's authorship applies to laws and generative conditions
- **Claim:** If an Architect exists, its authorship is understood as applying primarily to the laws, constants and generative conditions from which complexity became possible.
- **Type:** Interpretation
- **Concepts:** architect, process-oriented-architect, blueprint
- **Source:** § Preamble, The Architect and the Process (L18)

#### AD-P-008 — Evolution is a Blind Optimizer
- **Claim:** Evolution is a Blind Optimizer: a process without foresight, moral concern or predetermined biological destination.
- **Type:** Empirical
- **Concepts:** evolution, blind-optimization
- **Source:** § Preamble, The Architect and the Process (L18)

#### AD-P-009 — Humanity is a contingent product, not the culmination
- **Claim:** Humanity is one contingent product of the evolutionary process, not its culmination.
- **Type:** Interpretation
- **Concepts:** contingency-principle, anthropocentrism, evolution
- **Source:** § Preamble, The Architect and the Process (L18)

#### AD-P-011 — Humans are temporary, contingent and replaceable
- **Claim:** Humans are temporary, contingent and replaceable expressions of a universe that shows no evidence of privileging our species.
- **Type:** Inference
- **Concepts:** anthropocentrism, contingency-principle, rejection-of-ego
- **Source:** § Preamble, The Rejection of Cosmic Privilege (L20)

#### AD-P-012 — Cosmic insignificance does not entail personal or moral insignificance
- **Claim:** Cosmic insignificance does not entail personal or moral insignificance.
- **Type:** Methodological
- **Concepts:** anthropocentrism, meaning
- **Source:** § Preamble, The Rejection of Cosmic Privilege (L20)
- **Quote:** "Yet cosmic insignificance does not entail personal or moral insignificance."

## 9. Drift and open questions

- **AD-P-008 (Empirical):** the Preamble presents the Blind Optimizer as following ("therefore") from the Architect's authorship of laws. I typed it Empirical because the Book XII glossary says "Established science". "No predetermined biological destination" may instead be an interpretive claim.
- **AD-P-005 (Inference):** the Preamble states non-intervention as part of the deist inference. Chapter III separates "Observed Non-Management" from "Non-Interventionism", and Book XI lists "We should act as if no intervention will come" as a practical commitment. When Chapter III is distilled, check whether the types agree.
- **AD-P-010 (Not established):** the glossary calls the Rejection of Ego an "Axiom". That word suggests a methodological commitment rather than a denial for lack of evidence.
- **AD-P-015 (Methodological):** typed to match the glossary entry for Constructed Ethics ("Methodological commitment"). AD-P-016 is the chosen-foundation counterpart.
- **AD-P-018 (Interpretation):** this mixes an empirical base (organised matter and energy, energy conservation) with the interpretive framing of a "shared thermodynamic economy". I kept it as one Interpretation claim.
- **AD-P-034 (Interpretation):** this could be read as a chosen value rather than an interpretation.
- **Not distilled:** the closing rhetoric of L36 and L38 ("We do not claim certainty…", "What we offer instead…"), which repeats AD-P-035 and AD-P-013. It adds one phrase not captured elsewhere: "We do not require the universe to care about us before we care about one another."
- **IDs:** assigned in reading order. They are grouped by domain in section 2, so their numbering is not consecutive.
