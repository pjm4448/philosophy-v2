## 1. Thesis

The fine-tuned constants point to an Architect who cared about the rules (laws and initial conditions), not the outcomes. This process-oriented model explains both the precision of design and the observed absence of intervention, prayer-answering and correction of suffering. Non-intervention is the framework's most confident working assumption. Suboptimal biology and purposeless evolution follow from a designer of the process rather than of organisms. The question of what created the Architect is acknowledged as unresolvable from within the system.

### Evolution and biology

#### AD-III-014 — Biological systems contain apparent flaws
- **Claim:** Biological systems contain apparent flaws, such as cancer susceptibility, genetic decay and the choking hazard created by shared respiratory and digestive pathways.
- **Type:** Empirical
- **Concepts:** suboptimal-design
- **Source:** § III.4 Suboptimal Design (L267)

#### AD-III-015 — Flaws are not bugs but consequences of blind optimisation
- **Claim:** These flaws are not bugs in the traditional sense, since there is no intended function to deviate from; they are consequences of blind optimisation.
- **Type:** Interpretation
- **Concepts:** suboptimal-design, blind-optimization
- **Source:** § III.4 Suboptimal Design (L269)

#### AD-III-016 — Evolution selects for reproduction, not comfort or longevity
- **Claim:** Evolution optimises for survival and reproduction, not comfort or longevity, so features that do not prevent reproduction persist even when they cause suffering; to a blind filter, "good enough" is indistinguishable from "perfect".
- **Type:** Empirical
- **Concepts:** evolution, blind-optimization, suboptimal-design, suffering
- **Source:** § III.4 Suboptimal Design (L271–L273)

#### AD-III-019 — Variation and selection improve local fit
- **Claim:** Through random variation and environmental selection, evolution improves organisms' fit to local conditions.
- **Type:** Empirical
- **Concepts:** evolution
- **Source:** § III.5 Evolution (L281, L302)

#### AD-III-021 — Evolution is optimisation without purpose
- **Claim:** Evolution is optimisation without purpose: there is no target species or intended outcome, any organism that survives and reproduces is equally valid, and "better" means only more likely to survive locally.
- **Type:** Empirical
- **Concepts:** evolution, blind-optimization
- **Source:** § III.5 Evolution (L283–L288)
- **Restates:** AD-P-008

#### AD-III-023 — Evolution merely filters
- **Claim:** Evolution does not debug toward an intended function or refine toward a final version; it merely filters, because there is no intended function or final version.
- **Type:** Empirical
- **Concepts:** evolution, blind-optimization
- **Source:** § III.5, The Metaphor and Its Limits (L294)
- **Quote:** "It merely filters: variants that survive reproduce; variants that don't, don't."

#### AD-III-024 — Humans are not the goal of evolution
- **Claim:** Humans are not the goal or the point; they are one temporary output of a blind process that will continue long after them.
- **Type:** Methodological
- **Concepts:** anthropocentrism, rejection-of-ego, contingency-principle
- **Source:** § III.5, The Implication (L300)
- **Quote:** "We are not the goal. We are not the point."
- **Restates:** AD-P-010

#### AD-III-025 — Evolution will continue, but toward nothing
- **Claim:** If anything survives, the evolutionary filtering process will continue, but not toward anything.
- **Type:** Inference
- **Concepts:** evolution, blind-optimization
- **Source:** § III.5, The Implication (L300)
- **Quote:** "It will simply continue."

## 9. Drift and open questions

- **Non-intervention typing (candidate foundation-internal tension, no ledger entry opened):** Chapter III types non-intervention as a "working assumption, not … a certainty" (AD-III-010, Methodological). Book XI.5 agrees ("We should act as if no intervention will come", a practical commitment). The Preamble (AD-P-005) and Chapter I (AD-I-019), however, present non-intervention as part of the deist inference (Inference). Validation flags the AD-III-010 → AD-I-019 link for this reason.
- **AD-III-007 (Empirical):** the chapter introduces the list with "We observe". "No answers to prayers" and "no external enforcement of moral codes" are therefore typed as observations, although Book XI.1 does not list them among its empirical foundations.
- **AD-III-003 (Interpretation):** the chapter calls the process-oriented characterisation a "coherent model"; Book XI.2 lists it under Metaphysical Interpretations.
- **AD-III-009 (Postulated):** this follows Book XI.3, which lists functional indifference as a "Functional model" among the Postulated Models.
- **AD-III-015** combines the empirical point (flaws come from blind optimisation) with an interpretive one (there is no intended function). I kept them as one Interpretation claim; it could be split.
- **AD-III-025 (Inference):** this is a forward-looking statement drawn from evolution's lack of a target.
- **Domain heading "Evolution and biology"** was added because the template has no heading for evolution. Consider adding it to the template.
- **Not distilled separately:** L296 ("A parasitic worm is as 'optimized' as a philosopher…") is rhetoric restating AD-III-021, and is recorded under metaphors.
